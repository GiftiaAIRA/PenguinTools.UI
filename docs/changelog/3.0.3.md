同步上游企鹅工具箱 3.1.4、3.1.5 以及之后的 C2S 兼容性修复，内容与原版 3.0.5 对应。

Syncs upstream PenguinTools 3.1.4, 3.1.5 and the C2S compatibility fixes that followed, matching the original app's 3.0.5.

## 改进 / Improvements

- 加快大型谱面的转换和导出，包括 AIR、SLIDE 和变速较多的谱面\
  Convert and export large charts faster, including charts with many AIR or SLIDE notes and speed changes

## 修复 / Fixes

- C2S 长条和教程谱面的连击数与游戏一致\
  Align C2S combo counts for long notes and tutorial charts
- 谱面无法导出时，指出无效的 BPM 和判定设置所在的位置\
  Point to invalid BPM and judgement settings when a chart cannot be exported
- MGXC 保留 AIR-HOLD 的自定义高度，导出 C2S 时写成同轨 AIR-SLIDE\
  Preserve custom AIR-HOLD heights in MGXC and export them as same-lane AIR-SLIDE in C2S
- C2S 读写时保留 Heaven Hold 和 Air Solid（`ASO`、`HHD`、`HHX`）\
  Preserve Heaven Hold and Air Solid (`ASO`, `HHD`, `HHX`) when reading and writing C2S

## 变更 / Changes

- 含 `ASO`、`HHD` 或 `HHX` 的 C2S 提取为 MGXC 或 UGC 时改为报错。Margrete 没有对应的物件；此前会跳过这些物件并给出警告，转回 C2S 后它们会丢失\
  Extracting a C2S that contains `ASO`, `HHD`, or `HHX` to MGXC or UGC now reports an error. Margrete has no equivalent notes; these objects were previously skipped with a warning and lost when converting back to C2S
- 内置命令行工具更新为 3.1.5，并更新前端和 Tauri 依赖\
  Bundle the CLI 3.1.5 and update frontend and Tauri dependencies
